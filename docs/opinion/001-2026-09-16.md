# Opinion 001

**16 September 2026.** Written after the link programme shipped: mentions,
DataForSEO connector, two-number risk assessment, asset strategist.

**One line:** an exceptional diagnostic and a good technical fix generator,
with no programme around it. Do not sell it as an agency replacement yet.

---

## What was true at this date

53 agents, 8 missions, 60 tools, 21 connectors, 90 checks in the TypeScript
catalogue, 43 tables, 141 Python tests plus 32 engine tests. Live on Cloudflare
at a workers.dev subdomain. Accounts built but not provisioned: no D1 database
bound, no Google OAuth client, so sign-in reports what is missing and the audit
runs anyway.

## What would go well if it were sold tomorrow

The audit is genuinely good and the honesty is genuinely differentiating.
Nobody else in the category says "not measured" on a score. Nobody else catches
that a pixel tool's fixes are invisible to AI crawlers. The link verification
is better than tools charging $129 a month, for a mechanical reason: `rel` is
an HTML attribute and reading a page as text destroys it before the check runs.

A founder running it on their own site would get real value in the first ten
minutes. That is not nothing, and most tools in this category cannot say it.

## Where people get stuck

**The first run, at the connection step.** The biggest one. The buyer is a
founder, not an SEO. They hit "Connect Search Console" and face a Google
consent screen naming scopes. Some fraction bounce. Of those who connect, many
find their GSC property is the wrong variant (`http://` against `https://`,
`www` against not), see no data, and conclude the tool is broken.

**The approval queue at scale.** A real site produces 150 findings. The queue
asks for 150 decisions. Founders do not make 150 decisions. They make three and
close the tab.

**Publishing, for anyone not on WordPress.** WordPress is roughly 43% of the
web, so more than half of prospects cannot publish anything. They get a copy
button, which is the exact failure the product claims to fix.

**No model key.** Drafting is the most impressive feature and it is gated
behind a key most founders do not have.

## Why visibility might not move in three to four weeks

The part most likely to cause churn.

**Three to four weeks is too short and the product implies otherwise.** Google
takes weeks to recrawl and longer to re-rank. A fixed title on a page ranking
40th moves it to 35th, which produces no visible traffic change. The honest
window is three to six months. Every score in the app moves in days. Rankings
do not. That gap is where a customer decides the tool does not work.

**Technical fixes rarely move rankings on their own.** Alt text, schema, meta
descriptions: hygiene. They do not create demand or authority. A site that
ranks poorly usually ranks poorly because nobody links to it and its content is
thinner than the competition. The audit is excellent at hygiene, and hygiene is
the thing that moves the needle least.

**Small sites have no data.** A site with 158 clicks a month has no signal.
Any change sits inside the noise.

**Content can only be briefed, not fixed.** The highest-leverage change for
most sites is "this page is worse than the three above it". The product can say
so and write a brief. Someone still has to write the thing.

**Nothing here creates links.** It finds, qualifies, drafts and verifies. A
person still sends and waits.

## Blind spots at this date

1. **No expectation management.** Nothing tells the user what to expect and
   when. This will cause more churn than any missing feature.
2. **No competitive content analysis.** Rivals are compared on schema and
   crawler access, not on whether their page answers the query better.
3. **No internal linking execution.** Orphans are identified. Building the
   listing page that fixes 28 of them at once is manual.
4. **Local SEO is drafts only.** For a local business, the most valuable thing
   is the thing that cannot be done.
5. **Nothing runs unattended.** The pitch is "it happens without you". It
   happens when you click Run.
6. **No way to say "this is done".** No project state, no sense of progress, no
   "you have handled the critical findings, here is next month".

## The blunt version

An exceptionally good **diagnostic** and a good **fix generator** for technical
work. Not yet a **programme**: something that runs for six months, sets
expectations, and compounds.

An agency's value is roughly 30% knowing what is wrong and 70% doing it every
week for a year. The 30% is nailed. The 70% is started.

**Positioning recommendation at this date:** do not sell "replaces your SEO
agency". Sell "the audit and the fixes an agency charges £2,000 for, in ten
minutes, free", and grow into the retainer claim once scheduling and reporting
exist. That is true today, defensible, and it does not set up a refund
conversation in month two.
